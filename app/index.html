    Pads: click or keys 1–9 (Shift = soft). Click a track name or pad to make it active (library + adds voices to it); select a voice chip to shape it with the drum macros (double-click a slider to reset it). Each track can hold several voices: fixed uses the first, cycle steps through them per hit, random picks one. Track length sets polymeter. Shift-drag a step to move it, Alt-drag to copy it (all its settings). Accent = full velocity; Slide = legato into the next note (tie over empty steps). Ringing notes keep their voice when the next voice loads, so tails layer.

    Each MIDI channel can get its own library voice (+ in the library adds to the active channel). The FM-1 has one voice at a time, so the player switches voices between notes with parameter diffs — held notes keep their sound. Channel 10 can drive the drum kit from the Drums tab.

    Every 20 ms of speech becomes one FM-1 note with its own voice: three carriers tuned to the vowel formants (algorithm 22), or feedback noise for hiss. Typed text is stitched from sound units analysed from a text-to-speech voice; a recording is analysed directly. Intonation comes from the grammar: a part-of-speech tagger picks the accented words (nouns strongest, then adjectives, adverbs, verbs; shading shows the strength, hover for details), and each phrase turns on its last accent — falling for statements, rising for yes/no questions and before commas. Noun/verb pairs get their own stress (a REcord / to reCORD). Plays on the FM-1 or on the Software FM-1 (Out menu).
